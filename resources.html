Where to start

Two organisations whose material shaped this resource. Stonewall leads in the UK, PFLAG in the US. Both are worth knowing.

United States PFLAG

The largest organisation for LGBTQ+ people, their parents, families and allies. Strong on practical language and family support.

pflag.org ↗
United Kingdom Stonewall

The UK's leading LGBTQ+ rights charity. Best on workplace inclusion, trans allyship and disability intersection.

Books & film

A deliberately global shelf. These works speak from different continents, languages, and experiences, not the usual Western canon.

Books

  • Akwaeke Emezi — Dear SenthuranIgbo-Tamil author on being non-binary and the limits of Western frameworks

One thing you could do

Not a checklist. Pick one. Start there.

Takes a minute

Add your pronouns to your email signature. Not because you have to. Because it normalises it for people who do.
Stop saying "hey guys" to mixed groups. Try "everyone," "folks," "all." Takes a week to unlearn.
If someone shares their pronouns with you, use them. If you slip, correct yourself and move on. The over-apology puts the burden back on them.

This week

If you hear something, say something. Not a lecture. One sentence: "I don't think that one lands." The discomfort is yours to hold, not theirs.
When an LGBTQ+ person of colour has said something well, share it and credit them. Don't restate it as your own insight. Centre them.
Ask about someone's partner, not their "girlfriend" or "boyfriend." The word you choose says something about what you assume.

When you're ready

Read something by someone whose experience is further from your own than the sources you usually reach for. The global voices section is a start.
Notice who gets centred in the spaces you're in, including LGBTQ+ ones. Who's at the edge? Who's not there?
Allyship doesn't stop in July. The test is October, February, the ordinary Tuesday when it would be easier not to.

Ally is a verb. Actions speak louder than words, so rather than trying to convince people you are on their side, just be there.
